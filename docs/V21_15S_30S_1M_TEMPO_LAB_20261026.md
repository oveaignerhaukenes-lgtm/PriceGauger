# V2.1 Tempo-lab — 15s/30s/1m, reversal-latency research

## Purpose
Test whether the known 1m MACD+Stoch V2.1 weakness in contracting, short-cycle
markets is responsive to shorter *fully closed* bars, without modifying LIVE
strategy, Saxo orders, account arming, capital or safety gates.

Analyse -> V2.1 Tempo-lab (read-only):
- One exact subscribed Saxo Tech100 UIC, from historical actual
  \`lsim_hyper_quotes_1s\` received bid/ask records.
- Native epoch-aligned 15s, 30s and 60s OHLC from quote mid; each bar requires
  enough independent real samples and quote coverage with <=5s intrabar gaps.
- Every decision based on fully completed bars. Hypothetical fills are priced
  at real observed ASK for buy / BID for sell, strictly after bar completion
  plus a hypothetical extra latency scenario (0/1/3/5 seconds).
- No fake half-minute candles from stored 1m bars; never use intrabar quote
  not yet received at time t for strategy decisions.

## Six predeclared scenarios
3 cadences x 2 execution policies at one selected latency:
- **FLAT-first**: current V2.1 closed-bar, confirm and anti-churn policy.
  Opposite signal first targets zero, later newly confirmed bar may enter.
- **Direct-net flip 1bar PAPER**: on a fully closed bar with composite
  strength above the existing entry threshold in the *opposite* direction,
  propose a net position change from -0.02 to +0.01, etc., as one PAPER
  market order at the next eligible observed ask/bid.

Direct flip is **not** an instruction to skip LIVE safety or ignore
Saxo order errors: actual live direct-net reversal would still require
synchronous broker inventory, pending-order dedupe, account-specific netting
semantics, cap/margin limit, order response and fill reconciliation.

The two policies do not only change *execution*: 1bar direct flip enters a
new opposite-side position before V2.1's required 2-bar confirmation.
A positive result therefore cannot separate the benefit of reduced delay
from increased false reversals without a subsequent factorial experiment.

## Metrics and important caveats
- Log count, quality, completed bars, trade changes, quote age, missing
  observation/gap segments, max marked drawdown and P/L in index points × lots.
- Do NOT convert points×lots directly to NOK without Saxo product
  multiplier and FX; do not call PAPER mark-to-market real Saxo profit.
- Bid/ask crossing modeled; neither simulated market impact/slippage,
  commission, CFD funding, broker minimum lot validation, actual price
  acceptance nor latency of the production order/reconcile cycle measured.
- Tests with unclosed positions at missing-history boundary have no
  finalized total P/L: separately show mark at observed liquidation side.
  This avoids reporting a favorable-only closed trade total.
- 15s retains indicator periods in **number of candles**, so MACD slow
  lookback covers 6.5 minutes, 30s covers 13 minutes, and 1m covers 26.
  This is *not* an isolation of pure decision-time reduction. Compare same
  effective real-time lookbacks as a follow-up if 15s looks promising.
- Historical quotes may exist only since the PAPER collector was enabled.
  The simple source-level quote-quality filter fails closed on malformed
  rows; no silent interpolation or assumed 1Hz arrival guarantee.

## Second user concept: manager by observed returns vs regime classifier
Candidate future work only, not deployed/armed:
1. Use shared trustworthy source: flow-adjusted Saxo NAV + actual closed
   orders, fees and financing per strategy period, or pure portfolio-PAPER
   per common clock; do **not** compare 'open P/L' or percentages with
   different start times/capital.
2. Evaluate four *genuinely different* strategy return streams over
   identical history, measure pairwise return/exposure correlation,
   drawdown, sample size, trading-cost drag and forward calibration.
3. PAPER capital allocation with fixed global risk budget, max concentration
   per correlated cluster, finite minimum evidence, damped updates,
   turnover penalty, and a cash/FLAT option if all lack evidence.
4. Compare fixed equal-weight, winner-following and rhythm-aware manager
   in out-of-sample walk-forward; current winners may be highly correlated
   or reverse simultaneously.
5. Only after prospective account reconciliation, cost and robustness
   evidence consider user-authorized LIVE capital changes. The manager
   itself must never bypass manual controls or place broker orders.

## Release gates
- CI unit/regression tests for 15/30/60 and two flip policies, no-future,
  bid/ask and stale/gap refusal.
- Read-only UI confirms UIC and saved quote count; real Saxo raw history
  cannot be inspected through Railway management or GitHub connectors
  directly. Do not claim real profitability until a PAPER run on the app.
- No LIVE route, strategy key, worker, broker path, schema or arm change.
